Live Observation Feed

This feed contains machine-generated public-source observations. It is not the human-reviewed OpenVA catalog.

Observation events may indicate that a public source was fetched, redirected, unavailable, hash-changed, bot-protected, rate-limited, or flagged for review. Observation events are not vendor approval, risk findings, compliance conclusions, procurement recommendations, legal opinions, or materiality determinations.

Live observations are machine-generated public-source facts. They are not canonical catalog records and are not legal, compliance, procurement, security, KYC/AML, sanctions, or vendor-risk determinations.

Local Matcher

Your CSV is processed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to OpenVA.

Browser-local processing only. Select a CSV from your computer to match against OpenVA public metadata already loaded by this page. The file is parsed in browser memory only. OpenVA does not receive, store, persist, or transmit your private vendor inventory.
