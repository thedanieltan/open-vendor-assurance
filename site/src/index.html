Find public vendor assurance links in seconds.

OpenVA helps DPOs, procurement teams, and compliance operators find public vendor references such as privacy notices, trust centers, DPA pages, security pages, and subprocessor lists — without uploading private vendor data.

Recommended first step

Search by vendor name, legal entity, or domain. Open a record to review public references before starting your internal due diligence workflow.

Important boundary. OpenVA indexes public-source metadata only. It does not approve, certify, score, recommend, or determine whether any vendor is compliant, secure, suitable, or legally adequate. Do not upload private agreements, SOC reports, credentials, customer evidence, or confidential vendor inventories.

Developer view

Observation Feed

This feed contains machine-generated public-source observations. It is separate from the human-reviewed OpenVA catalog.

Observation events may indicate that a public source was fetched, redirected, unavailable, hash-changed, bot-protected, rate-limited, or flagged for review. Observation events are not vendor approval, risk findings, compliance conclusions, procurement recommendations, legal opinions, or materiality determinations.

Local Matcher

Your CSV is processed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to OpenVA.

Browser-local processing only. Select a CSV from your computer to match against OpenVA public metadata already loaded by this page. The file is parsed in browser memory only. OpenVA does not receive, store, persist, or transmit your private vendor inventory.

Accepted columns

Use whichever columns you have. At least one of vendor_name, business_entity_name, domain, or registration_number is required per row.
