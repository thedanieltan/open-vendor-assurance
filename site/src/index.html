Public vendor assurance source resolver

Turn vendor names into public assurance source URLs.

OpenVA helps DPOs, CISOs, procurement teams, compliance operators, and agents resolve vendor identities to public source references such as privacy notices, trust centers, DPA pages, security pages, and subprocessor lists — without uploading private vendor data.

Resolve a local CSV Search the reference cache

No account required. Public-source locator metadata only.

Recommended first step

Start with a vendor list or search by vendor name, legal entity, or domain. OpenVA returns public source references for your own internal review workflow.

Important boundary. OpenVA resolves public-source metadata only. It does not approve, certify, score, recommend, or determine whether any vendor is compliant, secure, suitable, or legally adequate. Do not upload private agreements, SOC reports, credentials, customer evidence, or confidential vendor inventories.

What OpenVA helps resolve

Public source references used in vendor intake

OpenVA is a source-location starting point for evidence gathering, not a substitute for legal, procurement, security, privacy, or risk review.

Developer view

Observation Feed

This feed contains machine-generated public-source observations. It is separate from the reviewed OpenVA reference cache.

Observation events may indicate that a public source was fetched, redirected, unavailable, hash-changed, bot-protected, rate-limited, or flagged for review. Observation events are not vendor approval, risk findings, compliance conclusions, procurement recommendations, legal opinions, or materiality determinations.
