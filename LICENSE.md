Documentation is intended to be licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International unless otherwise stated.

This repository does not grant rights to vendor source materials and does not mirror raw vendor documents by default.

## Practical rule

OpenVA licenses its own code, documentation, and factual metadata. It does not relicense vendor materials.
