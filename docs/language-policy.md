# Language Policy

The native-language source remains authoritative.

OpenVA may include English convenience summaries, but English summaries do not replace the native source or native interpretation.

## Requirements for non-English sources

For non-English sources, records should preserve where practical:

- native title;
- source language;
- native factual summary;
- English convenience summary;
- translation or summary method;
- review status.

## AI-assisted language handling

AI-assisted translation or summarisation is permitted only when:

1. the source language is recorded;
2. the output is marked as derived metadata;
3. no legal or compliance conclusion is introduced;
4. the original public source remains linked;
5. native-language meaning is not discarded.

## Jurisdiction-specific caution

Legal and regulatory terms may not map one-to-one across languages. Contributors must not flatten native legal terminology into advisory English conclusions.
