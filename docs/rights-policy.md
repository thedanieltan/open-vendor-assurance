# Rights Policy

OpenVA is metadata-first and does not mirror raw vendor documents by default.

## Rights classes

P1/P2 schemas will formalize rights classes. P0 recognizes these intended categories:

- `metadata_only`: store metadata, source URL, provenance, and hashes only;
- `public_link_only`: record source link and descriptive metadata only;
- `snapshot_allowed`: source may be retained if redistribution rights are clear and maintainer-approved;
- `snapshot_forbidden`: source must not be retained;
- `gated_excluded`: source requires restricted access and is out of scope.

## Default rule

When redistribution rights are unclear, use metadata plus hashes and do not store the raw document.

## Vendor ownership

Vendor documents, pages, PDFs, policies, marks, and original materials remain the property of their respective owners.

OpenVA does not relicense vendor source materials.
