# Supporting OpenVA

OpenVA is free and open source. Sponsorship helps fund catalog expansion, public-source verification, infrastructure, and ongoing maintenance across the browser, API, MCP, export, and automation surfaces.

Sponsorship is voluntary. It does not affect access to the catalog, software, source-reference data, public features, or the right to fork and build upon OpenVA.
