# Vendor Contributions

OpenVA welcomes vendor-submitted corrections and updates when they improve public-source factual metadata.

Vendor participation is useful because vendors often know when their public DPA, subprocessor list, trust center, privacy notice, AI terms, or security pages have moved or changed.

## What vendor contributors may submit

Vendor contributors may submit:

- corrected vendor identity metadata;
- updated public source URLs;
- updated public artifact references;
- corrected official domains;
- corrected source language metadata;
- new public source references;
- removal or deprecation notes for public sources that moved or were retired.

## What vendor contributors must not submit

Vendor contributors must not submit:

- private customer agreements;
- negotiated or bespoke terms;
- NDA-gated materials;
- customer portal exports;
- private trust-center documents;
- private SOC reports;
- private certificates;
- sales claims;
- risk conclusions;
- approval claims;
- claims that a vendor is compliant, safe, adequate, suitable, approved, recommended, or low risk.

## Vendor-submitted does not mean verified

A vendor-submitted pull request is not automatically accepted.

Maintainers still review:

- whether the source is public;
- whether the domain is official or covered by an approved publisher exception;
- whether the record is factual and non-advisory;
- whether the record avoids promotional claims;
- whether generated indexes are updated;
- whether the contribution follows repository schemas and policies.

## Public-source requirement

If a vendor wants OpenVA to reference a material, the material must be public.

If the material requires login, customer status, NDA, sales approval, support ticket access, private portal access, or anti-bot bypass, OpenVA may not include it.

## Representation boundary

Vendors may correct factual metadata about themselves.

Vendors may not use OpenVA to certify, rate, approve, recommend, or market themselves.

OpenVA records remain public metadata references, not vendor attestations or endorsements.
